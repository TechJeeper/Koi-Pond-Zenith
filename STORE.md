## Description

Paste into each platform asset collection (max 1600 characters):

```
Koi Pond Zenith is a calm top-down pond where two PixelLab koi swim the rim like clock hands.

White koi marks the hour. Orange koi marks the minute. An optional lily on the ring tracks the seconds.

• Zen koi-pond analog time
• White = hour · Orange = minute
• Optional seconds lily
• Soft digital time + optional date
• Swim clockwise or counter-clockwise
• Adjustable swim-ring inset
• Clay settings on your phone

Color watches show the full teal pond and orange koi; B&W watches keep the silhouette readout.
```

*(~520 characters)*
